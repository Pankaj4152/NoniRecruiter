# NoniRecruiter Candidate & User Guide

Welcome to **NoniRecruiter** — the adaptive technical interview platform. This guide explains how candidate interviews work, what to expect in the room, and how to use the interactive tools during your session.

---

## 1. Entering Your Interview Mission

When a recruiter sends you an interview link (`/invite/[sessionId]`), you will see your mission briefing:
- **Target Role & Company Name**
- **Scheduled Duration** (5, 10, or 15 minutes)
- **Candidate Profile & Uploaded Resume Summary**

Click **"Start Interview Quest"** to enter the live interview room.

---

## 2. The Voice-First Interview Room

NoniRecruiter operates as a voice-first conversational room:

1. **Interviewer Question**: The AI Recruiter (Noni) will speak the question aloud using natural voice synthesis. Text streams live on screen.
2. **Turn Handoff**: Once Noni finishes speaking, the large central **"Press Mic to Speak Answer"** button will unlock.
3. **Voice Response**: Press the Mic button and speak your answer. Your speech will transcribe in real time. Click **"Stop & Submit Voice Answer"** when complete.
4. **Keyboard Fallback**: If you prefer typing or your microphone is unavailable, click the **"Text Input"** button in the header bar to open a standard typing pad.

---

## 3. Using the Live Code Sandbox

During technical or coding phases (`CODING_CHALLENGE`), a **"Code Sandbox"** button will highlight in the header:

1. Click **"Code Sandbox"** to open the embedded editor modal.
2. Select your preferred programming language (**TypeScript**, **JavaScript**, **Python**, **C++**).
3. Click **"Run Code"** to execute your code in an isolated Docker/Piston sandbox. View `STDOUT` and `STDERR` execution output live.
4. Once verified, click **"Submit Code Solution"** to attach your code and execution logs directly to the interview conversation.

---

## 4. GitHub Repository Grounding

If your recruiter enabled **GitHub Repository Grounding**, Noni will ask you specific architectural follow-up questions about actual design patterns, tools, and code implementations found in your public GitHub repository.

---

## 5. Candidate Integrity & Fair Evaluation

NoniRecruiter is designed to evaluate technical depth, problem-solving logic, and trade-off understanding fairly:
- **Resume Grounding**: Unlisted skills mentioned in conversation are treated as positive expansion evidence (`💡 Expanded on resume during interview`).
- **Anti-Cheat Audit**: Avoid pasting large external code chunks directly into text fields; use the **Code Sandbox** for live coding challenges.

---

## 6. Interview Completion & Hiring Report

Once your interview duration is complete:
- Noni will thank you and close the session automatically.
- An evidence-backed **Final Interview Report** will be generated for human recruiter review, featuring competency scores, fact-checking verifications, and transcript evidence quotes.
